Our Story

Simplifying Compliance Since Day One

Conformity Alliance was founded with a singular mission — to eliminate the burden of regulatory complexity for Indian businesses. We recognized that enterprises, from ambitious start-ups to established conglomerates, often struggle with the labyrinth of labour laws, environmental regulations, and statutory obligations that govern business operations in India.

Our founding team of Advocates, Chartered Accountants, and Company Secretaries came together to build a single-window compliance platform under the framework of "Ease of Doing Compliance." Today, we provide end-to-end services spanning registrations, licensing, audits, payroll processing, litigation management, and departmental liaison — ensuring our clients achieve zero non-compliance status.

Under the expert stewardship of Advocate Pankaj Kumar, our Principal Consultant and a recognized authority in corporate governance and labour law, we have successfully served clients across industries including Logistics, Construction, E-Commerce, Retail, Food, Fintech, Manpower, Export, and Manufacturing.

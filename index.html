Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions regarding labour law, compliance audits, and registrations.

Most commercial establishments require a Shop and Establishment License, PAN/TAN, GST, and Professional Tax registration. If you employ 10 or more individuals, ESI registration is typically mandatory; for 20 or more, EPF registration is required. Manufacturing setups also need Factory Act licences, pollution NOCs, and fire clearances.
We have an established network of legal associates and liaison specialists across 28 Indian States and Union Territories. We centralize your compliance dashboard so you receive a unified reporting system while local state rules (e.g. S&E, Professional Tax, Labour Welfare Fund) are executed strictly per state bylaws.
Our senior advocates represent your organization directly, assist in preparing required statutory registers (Forms A through E, wage slips, inspection book), review departmental observations, and draft formal, legally sound responses to show-cause notices.
With all required architectural plans, NOCs, and contractor agreements vetted beforehand, most state portals process Contract Labour (CLRA) licenses within 7 to 14 business days, and Factory Act approvals within 15 to 30 business days.
